The problem

Fixed expiry dates ignore real-world conditions

Food spoilage and waste are major global challenges affecting food security, public health, and environmental sustainability. Current food safety practices rely on fixed expiry dates that only estimate shelf life - they don't reflect real-time storage conditions or actual chemical changes in food.

How it works

From chemistry to verdict in seconds

01

Passive indicator responds

A smart indicator built into the packaging continuously monitors for signs of spoilage - reacting to changes in your food the moment they start. No power required, always on.

02

The sensor picks it up

An ultra-sensitive sensor inside the packaging detects invisible spoilage signals - gases and chemical changes you can't see or smell - the moment they start happening.

03

NFC chip stores the result

A tiny wireless chip in the label stores the latest freshness reading. No battery required - it powers up instantly the moment your phone gets close.

04

Smartphone gives the verdict

Tap any NFC-enabled phone to the package. The app wirelessly reads the chip and returns an instant, accurate freshness verdict: Fresh, Consume Soon, or Spoiled.

Technology

The science inside

01 / Chemical

Targeted indicator dyes

Every food type spoils differently. Beyond Expiry uses a different smart indicator for each category - meat and fish, dairy, fresh produce, bakery, and dry goods - each tuned to detect the exact chemical signals that matter for that food. One size doesn't fit all.

02 / Nano

Invisible sensors, incredible accuracy

The sensors inside every Beyond Expiry package are engineered at the nanoscale - so sensitive they catch the very first signs of spoilage, long before you'd notice anything wrong. They work entirely passively, require no power, and never wear out.

03 / Wireless

Passive NFC integration

A tiny wireless chip in the label stores the freshness reading and transmits it to your phone in under a second - no battery, no app download needed, no internet required. Just tap and know. Works with any modern smartphone.
